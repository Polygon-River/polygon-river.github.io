4. Advertising and tracking

The Game shows rewarded and interstitial ads served directly by Unity Ads. When you first start the Game, before any ad is loaded, it asks whether ads may be personalized. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalized: we pass your choice to Unity Ads (as a GDPR consent signal and as an opt-out of sharing/targeted advertising under US state privacy laws) and to Google Firebase (ad storage, ad user data and ad personalization set to "denied"). You can change this choice at any time in the Game: Settings → Privacy; the new choice applies immediately.

We advertise the Game on Facebook and Instagram. To measure these campaigns, the Game uses the Facebook SDK by Meta. It starts only if you allowed personalized ads in the Game's privacy choice; it then sends Meta app install and app open events and in-app purchase events (product, price, currency). It sends your advertising identifier only if tracking is allowed (on iOS: if you allowed it in the App Tracking Transparency prompt). If you decline, the Facebook SDK is not started and nothing is sent to Meta. Automatic event logging and Facebook login are switched off.

On iOS, after that question, the system App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether the Game may use your advertising identifier (IDFA) to track you across apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not available to the Game or to Unity Ads, and ads are not personalized by tracking. You can change this at any time in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android: Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads). Unity Ads also shows an information button inside ads with its own privacy controls. Buying "No Ads" in the Game removes interstitial ads.

5. How long we keep data

We keep your account and progress while your account exists. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. When you delete your account, your account and cloud progress are erased; purchase records may be kept as long as tax and accounting law requires.

6. Delete your account

In the Game: Settings → Delete Account, then confirm. This permanently erases your account and progress. You can also request deletion by email to szymonk134@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Reel Runner account" (include the Apple or Google account you sign in with); we process requests within 30 days and confirm by email. Details: Support — delete account.

Analytics data linked to your player ID is deleted automatically after the retention period in section 5, or earlier if you ask us by email. Advertising data collected by Unity Ads is controlled by Unity as an independent controller; to access or delete it, contact Unity as described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Campaign measurement data sent to Meta is controlled by Meta; you can manage it in your Facebook or Instagram settings (Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Your activity off Meta technologies) or as described in the Meta Privacy Policy. To stop ad tracking on your device, reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR in the EU/UK, CCPA/CPRA in California) you have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. We do not sell personal information for money. Showing personalized ads may count as "sharing" for targeted advertising under some US state laws; you can opt out at any time with the in-game choice in Settings → Privacy (decline personalized ads). To exercise your rights, email szymonk134@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Poland: Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, uodo.gov.pl).
