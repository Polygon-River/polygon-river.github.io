4. Advertising and tracking

The Game shows rewarded and interstitial ads. On iOS you are asked through the App Tracking Transparency prompt whether ads may use your advertising identifier; if you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalized by tracking. You can change this at any time in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android: Settings → Google → Ads). Buying "No Ads" in the Game removes interstitial ads.

5. How long we keep data

We keep your account and progress while your account exists. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. When you delete your account, your account and cloud progress are erased; purchase records may be kept as long as tax and accounting law requires.

6. Delete your account

In the Game: Settings → Delete Account, then confirm. This permanently erases your account and progress. You can also request deletion by email to [CONTACT_EMAIL] (include the Apple or Google account you sign in with); we process requests within 30 days. Details: Support — delete account.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR in the EU/UK, CCPA/CPRA in California) you have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. We do not sell personal information. To exercise your rights, email [CONTACT_EMAIL]. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Poland: Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, uodo.gov.pl).

8. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and save data on the device is encrypted.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version; important changes will be announced in the Game.
