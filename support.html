Frequently asked questions

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

Open Settings → Account and connect your account with Apple or Google. On the new phone, sign in with the same Apple or Google account and your progress is restored from the cloud.

My purchase didn't arrive

Restart the Game with an internet connection — pending purchases are delivered on start. If it is still missing, email us the purchase receipt (App Store / Google Play order ID). Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play.

How does energy work?

Starting a level costs 1 energy. Winning gives it back. Energy refills by 1 every 15 minutes, up to 5.

Can I see the odds of random rewards?

Yes. The skill slot machine shows its odds below the draw, and the LootBot machine and Ancient crates have an "i" button with the odds for every rarity.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language. Available: English, French, German, Italian, Polish, Portuguese, Spanish.

How do I delete my account?

In the Game: Settings → Delete Account, then confirm. This permanently erases your account and all progress and cannot be undone. Purchases are not refunded by deleting the account.

What is deleted: your player account, cloud save (levels, items, currencies, settings) and in-game purchase history. What is kept: records of real-money purchases (product, date, transaction ID) for as long as tax and accounting law requires; gameplay analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically.

Can't open the Game? Email szymonk134@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Reel Runner account" and the Apple or Google account you signed in with (or your device model and the approximate date you started playing). We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.
